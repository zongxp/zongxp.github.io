The MRI physics lab is situated within the School of Biomedical Engineering at ShanghaiTech University.
Our group members come from diverse educational backgrounds, including physics, BME, computer science, electrical engineering, robotics, and medicine.  We share the passion for better understanding the inner workings of the human body and improving the diagnosis and treatment of diseases.  In order to develop MRI technology that can have real impact on health care, we collaborate closely with engineers from United Imaging Healthcare and scientists in the hospitals.
